What you review

Evidence, not a black box.

TraceAI is designed to show where a lead came from and why it deserves attention.

  • Possible visual matches

    Images that may be visually relevant to the reference photos.

  • Source links

    Direct public source routes stay attached to the evidence lead.

  • Public context

    Review the source context around a possible visual lead.

  • No automatic identity claims

    TraceAI surfaces evidence leads; humans decide what the evidence means.

Responsible use

Built for careful review.

TraceAI helps discover and organise public visual evidence leads. It does not automatically determine identity, ownership, infringement, illegality, or whether content should be removed.
